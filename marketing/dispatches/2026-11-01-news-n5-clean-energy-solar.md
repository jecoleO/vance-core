### Key Grammar Points Covered
1. **Noun は Noun を Verb** (Topic [は] + Direct Object [を] + Verb) — *Subject performs action on object*
2. **Noun が Verb** (Subject [が] + Verb) — *Subject performs an intransitive action or exists*
3. **Noun で Place/Scope** (Noun [で] + Verb) — *In / at / by means of*
4. **Noun と Noun** (Noun + と + Noun) — *And (noun conjunction)*
5. **Na-Adj / Noun に する** (Na-adjective / Noun + に + する) — *To make something [adjective]*
