### Key Grammar Points Covered
1. **〜が〜します** (Subject noun + が + Verb) — *Subject performs an action*
2. **〜を〜します** (Object noun + を + Verb) — *Direct object of the action*
3. **〜の〜** (Noun 1 + の + Noun 2) — *Possession or modification (Noun 1's Noun 2)*
4. **〜に〜します** (Noun / na-Adjective stem + に + Verb / Copula) — *Direction, target, or adverbial state*
5. **〜と〜** (Noun 1 + と + Noun 2) — *Together with / And*
