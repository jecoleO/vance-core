### Headline
**各地の伝統料理を楽しむ食の祭典が東京で始まる**  
*Food festival celebrating regional traditional dishes opens in Tokyo*

### Reading Summary (English Translation)
> A large food festival featuring traditional dishes from various parts of Japan has begun at a Tokyo park. Many visitors have flocked to the venue, enjoying tasting local cuisine and buying specialty products.

The organizers hope people will learn about the appeal of regional areas through this event. A person who ate the dishes smiled and said, "It made me want to ride the bullet train and go to the local area."
