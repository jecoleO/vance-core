⚡ N2 High-Yield Grammar Quick Cheatsheet (Preview)

～をめぐって (o megutte)
Controversy / debate surrounding...
Formula: Noun + をめぐって. Always implies dispute or conflicting positions among multiple parties.
～ざるを得ない (zaru o enai)
Have no choice but to...
Formula: V-nai stem + ざるを得ない (する → せざるを得ない). Reluctant action forced by circumstance.
～にほかならない (ni hoka naranai)
Nothing other than; precisely because...
Formula: Noun / から + にほかならない. Emphatically states the single root reason or true nature.
～ものなら (mono nara)
If it were possible (unlikely hope) / If you dare...
Formula: Potential verb + ものなら. Expresses a wish for something nearly impossible or high consequence.
