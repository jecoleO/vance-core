Why Single-File Local-First?

The modern web is bloated with megabyte-heavy frameworks that fail the moment you lose cellular connection on the Osaka subway or on a flight. Every tool here is engineered as a standalone, zero-dependency HTML document that runs completely in your browser, saves its state locally, and can be saved to your device as a permanent, offline file.
