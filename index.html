Why Single-File Local-First?

The modern web is bloated with heavy software and frameworks that need a constant internet connection and loads of personal data. Every tool here is engineered as a standalone, zero-dependency HTML document that runs completely in your browser, saves its state locally, and can be saved to your device as a permanent, offline file. No tricks, just simple apps that work.
